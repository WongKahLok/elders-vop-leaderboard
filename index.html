Connect your sheet

  1. In the Google Sheet, click Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer.
  2. Copy the sheet's link from the address bar and paste it here.

The link is remembered on this computer. You can also put the sheet ID in CONFIG.sheetId at the top of the page's script, or add ?sheet=ID to the page address.
